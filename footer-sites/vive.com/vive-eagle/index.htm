Where can I try VIVE Eagle?

Where can I buy VIVE Eagle?

Find Taiwan stores
Does VIVE Eagle come in different sizes? Which size suits me the most?
VIVE Eagle is available in two sizes: M and L. We recommend visiting a store to find the best fit for you.
Do you need a smartphone to use VIVE Eagle?
Yes, a smartphone is necessary to use VIVE Eagle. To set up VIVE Eagle, you will need to download VIVE Connect (available on the Google Play Store or App Store) on your phone.
What's included in the VIVE Eagle box?
You’ll find the following items in the box included with your VIVE Eagle purchase:
  • VIVE Eagle AI glasses
  • Charging cable
  • Glasses case
  • Safety guide and warranty card
What is VIVE AI Plus?
VIVE AI Plus is the elite AI service for VIVE Eagle that includes: real-time enhanced AI responsiveness, locational search support, unlimited memory capacity, and other exclusive features. A 24-month free trial of VIVE AI Plus is included with your VIVE Eagle purchase.
Can VIVE Eagle get wet?
VIVE Eagle is water resistant but not waterproof. It is not intended for use in wet environments. Do not submerge your VIVE Eagle in liquid. Protect it from excess moisture to prevent damaging your glasses. In case your VIVE Eagle gets submerged in water, thoroughly dry the device without exposing it to heat. Once dry, make sure that the charging port, speakers, touchpad, and camera are functional. For any damages, refer to your VIVE Eagle warranty terms and conditions.
