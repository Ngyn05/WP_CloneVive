4. Diopter dials may be beneficial to near-sighted individuals but are not intended to replace corrective lenses or compensate for eye disorders.

5. Depth-sensing-enabled features are limited to indoor environments and are only available with software version 1.0.999.424 and up.

6. Content compatibility required. Compatible content and devices sold separately. PC VR titles are available on VIVE XR Elite via VIVE Streaming. System requirements must be met.

7. VIVE Ultimate Tracker requires a VIVE Wireless Dongle to connect with compatible devices, which currently include VIVE XR Elite, VIVE Focus 3, and, through our beta program, third-party PC VR headsets via SteamVR. VIVE Wireless Dongle and compatible devices sold separately. Each VIVE Wireless Dongle can connect with one to five (1–5) VIVE Ultimate Trackers.

8. Content compatibility required. Please check the specifications to ensure the content supports face/lip tracking functions. Eye surgery, eye disease, heavy makeup, and high myopia may affect eye-tracking performance. The performance of dynamic foveated rendering may vary based on the content and the application.

9. The VIVE Case for XR Series requires the battery cradle to be detached from the VIVE XR Elite and stored separately.
