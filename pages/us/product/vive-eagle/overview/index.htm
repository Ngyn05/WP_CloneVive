What is VIVE Eagle?
VIVE Eagle is HTC's lightweight AI smart glasses designed to enhance everyday life, work, and travel. Powered by compatible AI services, including large language model (LLM)-based capabilities, VIVE Eagle enables users to access supported AI features hands-free through voice commands or customizable shortcut buttons.

Key capabilities include:

1. AI assistance: Use voice commands and LLM-powered AI to ask questions, get helpful information, and complete everyday tasks hands-free.
2. Hands-free photo and video capture: Record what you see from a natural first-person perspective.
3. Open-ear audio: Listen through open-ear speakers with virtual bass and sound leakage reduction technology.
4. Visual and Live Translation: Translate printed text and signs in real time by simply looking at them.
5. AI-powered memory: Save and retrieve notes, lists, contacts, and other information using voice commands.
6. Privacy-focused design: Security and privacy safeguards help protect your data and give you greater control over your personal information.
What AI features does VIVE Eagle offer?
VIVE Eagle offers a range of AI-powered features designed to help users manage daily tasks, communicate across languages, capture information, and explore their surroundings.

Key AI-powered capabilities include:

1. Integrated AI assistant: Powered by large language model (LLM) technology to answer questions, support everyday tasks, and integrate with your smartphone assistant to help manage schedules using natural voice commands.
2. Visual translation: Translate written text simply by looking at it, with support for 70+ languages.
3. Real-time translation: Translate spoken conversations or continuous speech as it happens.
4. AI-powered memory: Create and retrieve lists, contacts, and notes using voice commands.
5. VIVE AI Notes: Record spoken content, generate transcripts, and create AI summaries for meetings and conversations.
6. Smart search: Get timely information such as sports scores, stock prices, and nearby places.
7. Custom AI button actions: Assign frequently used AI tasks to the AI button, such as identifying landmarks, plants, animals, or describing a scene.
How is VIVE Eagle different from other AI smart glasses?
VIVE Eagle stands apart from other AI smart glasses by combining flexible AI services, privacy-focused design, seamless smartphone integration, premium ZEISS optics, and productivity features in a lightweight everyday wearable. VIVE Eagle gives users greater flexibility by supporting different AI services depending on the region and device compatibility.

Key differentiators include:

1. Flexible AI services:
Depending on the region, VIVE Eagle supports AI services powered by Google Gemini or OpenAI GPT, giving users access to multiple AI ecosystems rather than relying on a single provider.
2. Privacy-focused design:
User queries are de-identified before being sent to supported AI services, and VIVE AI is certified to ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27701 information security and privacy management standards.
3. Seamless smartphone integration:
VIVE Eagle can activate supported smartphone assistants, including Siri and Google Assistant, allowing users to make calls, set reminders, manage calendars, play music, navigate, and perform other supported voice-activated phone functions.
4. Premium ZEISS optics:
VIVE Eagle is available with multiple ZEISS lens options, including UV400 Sunglass Lenses, UV400 Blue Light Blocking Lenses, and UV380 AdaptiveSun Lenses designed to adapt to changing light conditions.
5. VIVE AI Notes:
Capture meetings, classes, interviews, and conversations, then generate transcripts, AI-powered summaries, and speaker identification to help organize, review, and revisit important information.

Does VIVE Eagle support real-time translation?
Yes. VIVE Eagle supports real-time voice and image translation through two complementary AI-powered features.
1. Live Translation:

Translates spoken conversations and ongoing speech in real time. Users can select the other speaker's language as the input language and their preferred language as the output language. Live Translation includes both Conversation Mode for two-way conversations and Listening Mode for translating speeches, presentations, or other continuous audio. Live Translation currently supports 90+ languages.

2. Visual translation:
Translates printed text simply by looking at it through VIVE Eagle. Visual translation currently supports more than 70 languages, making it easy to understand signs, menus, documents, and other printed text while traveling or in everyday situations.

Translation quality may vary depending on factors such as the language pair, dialect, accent, background noise, image quality, lighting conditions, and internet connectivity. What can the VIVE Eagle camera capture, and what AI-powered camera features does it offer?
VIVE Eagle features a 12 MP ultra-wide camera that enables hands-free, first-person photo and video capture, along with AI-powered visual features that help users capture, understand, and explore the world around them.
Camera and capture features include:

1. First-person photo and video capture
• Capture high-resolution photos at 3024 × 4032 and record video at up to 3K resolution.
• Photos and videos are captured in a vertical format, making them ideal for social media sharing and short-form content.
• Video recording is limited to 3 minutes by default and can be extended to 10 minutes per recording.
• High-frame-rate slow-motion recording is supported, with playback speed adjustable later using a compatible smartphone.

2. AI-powered visual features
• Visual Translation: Instantly translate foreign-language text simply by looking at it through VIVE Eagle.
• Scene Recognition: Ask VIVE AI to describe your surroundings or help identify landmarks, objects, plants, animals, and other points of interest.
• Movement Alert: VIVE Eagle can detect excessive movement and alert you when camera motion may result in a blurry photo, helping reduce motion blur and improve image clarity.

Camera performance and AI-powered visual features may vary depending on lighting conditions, internet connectivity, region, language, and service availability. Certain AI features may require user permissions, a compatible smartphone, and a VIVE AI subscription. How do people know when VIVE Eagle is taking a photo or recording video?
VIVE Eagle is designed with privacy in mind. A visible capture indicator light illuminates whenever the camera is taking a photo or recording video to notify people nearby that image or video capture is in progress. The indicator light automatically adjusts its brightness based on ambient lighting conditions for better visibility. To provide an additional layer of privacy protection, the camera is automatically disabled when the glasses are not being worn or if the capture indicator is obstructed.

Users are responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations governing privacy, photography, and the recording of audio or video, including obtaining any required notices, permissions, or consents before capturing or recording others.

How long does the VIVE Eagle battery last, and how fast does it charge?
VIVE Eagle is powered by a built-in 235 mAh rechargeable battery designed to support everyday use. On a full charge, VIVE Eagle provides more than 36 hours of standby time, up to approximately 4.5 hours of continuous music playback, and more than 3 hours of continuous voice calling.

Its magnetic fast-charging system can charge the battery to approximately 50% in as little as 10 minutes and approximately 80% in about 23 minutes. VIVE Eagle can also be used while charging.

For extended use, the optional Power Boost magnetic charger weighs less than 8 grams and adds 120 mAh of battery capacity, increasing the total battery capacity from 235 mAh to 355 mAh for longer use while you're on the go.

Battery life and charging times are estimates based on typical usage and may vary depending on individual usage patterns, enabled features, device settings, network conditions, signal strength, ambient temperature, battery age, charging accessories, and other environmental factors.

Is VIVE Eagle waterproof or water resistant?
VIVE Eagle is rated IP54 for dust and splash resistance. It is designed to withstand incidental splashes but is not waterproof and should not be submerged in water or exposed to excessive moisture.

If VIVE Eagle comes into contact with water, remove it from the wet environment immediately and dry it thoroughly using a soft, dry cloth. Do not use heat or external drying devices. Before using the device again, ensure it is completely dry and verify that the charging contacts, speakers, touch controls, and camera are functioning properly.

If the glasses do not operate normally or appear damaged following exposure to liquid, please refer to the VIVE Eagle Limited Warranty or contact VIVE Support for assistance.
